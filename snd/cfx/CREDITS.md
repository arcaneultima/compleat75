# Compleat FX one-shots (snd/cfx)

All files here were trimmed, mixed down to mono and level-matched from these sources.

- Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0: Impact Sounds (pop1, pop2, crack, thud, tink1, tink2, rubble1, rubble2), Sci-Fi Sounds (gears, clang, clang2, servo, slab, doorC, doorO, boom, crunch, slime1, slime2, zap, ignite), RPG Audio (coins1, coins2, latch), Casino Audio (shake, dice1, dice2, die1, die2)
- adbertiser-sfx (GitHub), CC0 1.0, synthesized from code: static1, static2, glitch1, glitch2, subboom
- Recorded replacements for the old synthesized files:
  - "Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC, CC BY 4.0: lswitch, steam, stamp, coin, pdown, crunchS, rip, hitC, punch, wave, stream
  - CC0 via EternityForest/Free-SFX: rubberduckie (Freesound 219836, swell), rubberduck (shatter, bubbles), Owlish (paper1, paper2, flip, scrib1, scrib2), qubodup (whoosh, whooshB, rwhoosh reversed, swipe, flyby slowed), PagDev (fire)
  - CC0 via pbinkley/js-typewriter: monotraum typewriter keys (keys, print, arranged)
  - CC0 Freesound via Abhijeet-Ravan/sfx-audio-library: loganzsound 774635 (riser), Kodack 256862 (wind)
- Freesound, CC0 1.0: drone ("Void Monolith Subharmonic Drone" by bassimat, 854874), win ("Success Vibraphone Triad" by Qvantor, 878602), tvoff ("TV_Switch_Off" by SignatureSoundsOrg, 872647)
- Soundza (soundza.com), Soundza Standard License: tick ("Grandfather Clock Reverb"), klaxon ("Aircraft Alarm Klaxon")

The effects also reuse the game's own Kenney impacts in snd/ (bell, metal, glass, plate, soft).
